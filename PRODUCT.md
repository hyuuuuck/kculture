# K-Spot Now product direction

## Reader and purpose

Independent English-speaking visitors who want to understand and participate in culture in Korea. Start with a workable cultural day in Seoul; retain useful, explicitly separate regional coverage.

The reader should leave an article knowing what the experience means, whether it suits them, how language/entry conditions affect participation, the Korean place name and which unresolved condition changes the plan.

## Editorial model

Official records establish facts. Our contribution is explanation, a reasoned comparison, a practical sequence and honest limits. API collections stay private until an article and accountable review exist. Cultural interpretation must be supported, not invented; documentary research is not a claimed visit.

The user has requested a whole-site visual replacement. The incumbent blue/coral directory is an anti-reference, not a brand constraint. Keep real source-linked images and clear English. The homepage should help a reader understand a cultural experience and choose a useful article before offering dates and saved plans. Long-form articles must explain the subject, not merely describe how to verify another website. Do not replace substance with more badges, cards, scores or repetitive checklists.

## Redesign scope and acceptance

- Redesign all public template families: home, guide index and articles, event index and articles, calendar, saved plan, about and policies. Repainting the home or appending more CSS overrides is not the requested whole-site replacement.
- Prioritize the desktop reading and planning experience. Protect basic narrow-screen readability, navigation and saved-state actions; a separate mobile product is out of scope.
- Keep the reader's path explicit: understand the culture, compare relevant choices, then act using concrete participation information. Put source methodology in an accessible evidence area rather than repeating it as the principal content.
- Preserve route URLs, sourced facts, honest uncertainty, accessibility, saved items and ads-off behavior. New visual design is not permission to invent attendance, reservation results, image rights or responsible reviewers.
- Distinguish local implementation, deployed behavior and verified reader value. A passing build proves none of the latter by itself. Visual direction selection and the full-site build remain unfinished until explicitly completed and verified.

## Business model

AdSense is a later monetization layer. Keep ads off until approval and consent verification. Diagnose earnings using observed qualified readership, geography and actual RPM, not a promise based on page count.

## Explicit non-goals

- A Korea-wide automated directory or many thin city/tag pages.
- Generating articles to hit a publication or word quota.
- Claiming English onsite service merely because an English website exists.
- Refreshing editorial dates after keyword checks.
- Waiting for a particular Search Console count as if it were an AdSense rule.
- Repeated submissions without a material, verified change.

## Evidence and release

Automated checks verify integrity only. Human editorial review and an actual reader task check are our internal quality controls, not additional Google eligibility rules. Record real outcomes and unresolved problems against the revision fingerprint. Production verification follows authorized deployment; submission follows all required evidence and fresh account checks. Never fabricate either.
