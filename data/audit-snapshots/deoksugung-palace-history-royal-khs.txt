Page: Deoksugung Palace and Its History

The official page identifies Deoksugung Palace as the former Gyeongungung Palace. Its history records that Emperor Gojong declared the Korean Empire in 1897 and that Gyeongungung Palace then served as the imperial palace. It also records the 1907 renaming as Deoksugung Palace.
